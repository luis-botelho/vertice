## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Audience
The brand represents an institutional bridge between manufacturing giants and regional building material retailers across Angra dos Reis and Costa Verde, RJ. The personality conveys corporate reliability, direct trade clarity, operational solidity, and maritime-regional resonance. The target audience comprises hardware store owners, construction material distributors, purchasing directors, and factory sales leaders who value clear terms, structured catalog indexing, and precise wholesale quotes over consumer shopping frills.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Tone
The visual direction fuses **Corporate Modern** with **Technical Precision Minimalist**:
- **Structured Architectural Geometry:** Subtle references to the faceted triangle-chevron "V" motif serve as grounding anchors, directional accents, and watermarked document framing.
- **Institutional Confidence:** Crisp white surfaces, deep oceanic navy framing, and vibrant blue actionable points establish clear hierarchy without visual fatigue.
- **Enterprise Quotation Ergonomics:** Focus is shifted toward high-density data tables, clear commercial clauses, tax/freight breakdown units, and RFQ (Request for Quote) flows rather than impulsive consumer checkout patterns.

## Colors

The palette directly honors the formal brand asset guidelines, anchoring regional maritime identity within a clean corporate structure:

- **Primary (`#102B46` / `#0B2D4A`):** Deep Navy. Used for core structural framing, primary top-level headings, header bars, and authoritative table head columns.
- **Secondary (`#2F80ED` / `#3882F6`):** Vibrant Electric Blue. Drives interactive focus, primary quotation actions, selected tab states, and directional status indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#8DC7E8`):** Sky Cerulean Accent. Applied sparingly for active hover glows, badge backgrounds, subtle callout lines, and brand chevron pairings.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` / `#94A3B8`):** Cool Slate series. Governs secondary technical labels, breadcrumbs, table borders, and metadata typography.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F8FAFC` to `#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white background paired with cool slate-tinted canvas to maintain high contrast for legal and wholesale quote audits.

### Functional Mapping
- **Success:** `#059669` (Order approved, contract active).
- **Warning:** `#D97706` (Quote pending credit review, stock threshold low).
- **Destructive:** `#DC2626` (Order canceled, proposal expired).
- **Surface Borders:** `#E2E8F0` for subtle dividers; `#CBD5E1` for table row dividers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is engineered around dense, unambiguous commercial documents, technical spec sheets, and price negotiations:

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its modern geometric architecture and authoritative legibility. High-weight headers bring institutional prestige aligned with technical industrial brands.
- **Body & Data Tables (Inter):** Leverages neutral tall x-height and exceptional tabular numeric rendering (`tnum`). Numerical financial figures, product SKUs, tax percentages, and unit measurements align cleanly without optical staggering.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Strict uppercase tracking applied to technical flags (e.g., `CNPJ`, `TABELA FOB/CIF`, `ICMS-ST`) ensures distinct demarcation between data descriptors and actual values.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Philosophy
The platform employs a rigid 12-column fluid grid system with fixed gutters, optimizing both data consumption on desktop workstations and rapid catalog consultation on mobile field tablets.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with 32px gutters and 48px lateral page margin. Side-by-side split screens for catalog browsing and draft quote review.
- **Tablet / Laptop (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns with 24px gutters and 32px margin. Collapsible commercial parameter sidebars into top-level drawer modals.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px gutters and 16px margin. Vertical card transformation for tabular quotation manifests.

### Ergonomic Spacing Strategy
Spacing rhythm adheres to an 8pt base grid. Component paddings within quotation inputs, SKU rows, and client detail panels prioritize information density without crowding touch points, enabling rapid data verification by purchasing agents.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve institutional dignity and paper-like commercial document authority, elevation uses restrained tonal layering coupled with surgical, low-contrast ghost borders:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background neutral slate-50 (`#F8FAFC`). No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Pure white fill (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border in `#E2E8F0`. Layered with an ambient shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(16, 43, 70, 0.05), 0px 1px 2px rgba(16, 43, 70, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Popovers, Active Filters):** `0px 4px 6px -1px rgba(16, 43, 70, 0.08), 0px 2px 4px -1px rgba(16, 43, 70, 0.04)` wrapped in a 1px `#CBD5E1` outline.
- **Level 3 (Modal Quotation Finalization & Overlays):** `0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(16, 43, 70, 0.12), 0px 10px 10px -5px rgba(16, 43, 70, 0.04)` with backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`) over a `#102B46` 40% opacity overlay.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **Soft (`1`)** roundedness profile (4px base radius, 8px on large cards, 12px maximum on modals). 

- **Rationale:** Industrial building material catalogs, logistics invoices, and architectural hardware specifications benefit from structured rectangular clarity. Softening sharp corners to 4px–8px maintains a professional, software-engineered polish without drifting into casual consumer roundness.
- **The "V" Geometric Accent:** Section headings, quote summary banners, and hero modules can leverage a sharp 45-degree corner notch or subtle background SVG chevron watermark referencing the brand's dual-chevron emblem.

## Components

### 1. Primary & Secondary Action Buttons
- **Primary Action (Generate Proposal / Submit RFQ):** Solid `#102B46` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `border-radius: 4px`, `height: 40px`, padding `0 16px`. Active state transitions to `#2F80ED`.
- **Secondary Action (Export PDF / Download Spec):** Outlined 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#102B46`. Hover state introduces `#F1F5F9` background and `#2F80ED` border.
- **Tertiary Utility:** Text-only in `#2F80ED` with chevron indicator.

### 2. Commercial Quotation Tables
- **Header Row:** Deep Navy `#102B46` background, `#FFFFFF` text, uppercase `label-sm` typography with right-aligned numeric headers (`Qtd`, `Valor Unit.`, `IPI`, `ST`, `Total`).
- **Data Rows:** Alternating background (Zebra: `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`), bordered with 1px `#E2E8F0`. Tabular figures rendered with monospaced digit features enabled. Hover highlights row in `#F0F7FF`.
- **Summary Footer Block:** High-contrast right-aligned summary card with subtotal, freight type tags (`FOB`/`CIF`), tax additions, and prominent final wholesale total.

### 3. Form Input Fields & Filter Dropdowns
- **Height & Bounds:** `38px` input box, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 4px border radius.
- **Focus State:** 1px `#2F80ED` ring with `0 0 0 3px rgba(47, 128, 237, 0.15)`.
- **Institutional Context Labels:** Persistent micro-labels placed above fields with optional company tax flags (`CNPJ / Razão Social`, `Inscrição Estadual`).

### 4. B2B Commercial Proposal Status Badges (Chips)
- **Draft / Em Análise:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#64748B`, 1px border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Sent to Factory / Encaminhado:** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#2F80ED`, 1px border `#BFDBFE`.
- **Approved / Faturado:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#059669`, 1px border `#A7F3D0`.

### 5. Client & Line Card Representations
- **Supplier Representation Card:** Clean container with prominent factory badge, product category chips (e.g., *Tubos & Conexões, Louças, Metais*), commission terms metadata, and quick-add to proposal drawer.
- **Watermark Treatment:** Clean SVG rendition of the split V-chevron embedded at 4% opacity in the bottom-right corner of PDF preview viewports and presentation cards.
